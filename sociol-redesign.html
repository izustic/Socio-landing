04 / Good questions

Before you
say hello.

A few things you might be wondering about finding friends with Sociol.

What is Sociol?

Sociol is a social connection concept designed to help people discover compatible friends and build smaller, more meaningful circles.

Is this a dating app?

Sociol is focused on friendship and community, not romantic matching. The goal is to help you find people you can connect with in everyday life.

What if I’m introverted or a little shy?

You don’t need to be the loudest person in the room. Take your time, start with shared interests, and connect in a way that feels comfortable to you.
