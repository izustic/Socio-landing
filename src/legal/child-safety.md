# Sociol Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 26, 2026

## Our commitment to child safety

Sociol has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We prohibit content and behavior that sexually abuses, exploits, or endangers anyone under 18. These standards apply to everyone using Sociol, including profiles, photos, messages, Circles, and interactions arranged through the app.

## Prohibited content and behavior

Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) are strictly prohibited on Sociol. Users must not create, upload, possess for sharing, request, distribute, or link to CSAM through our service.

- CSAM includes images, videos, and computer-generated depictions of the sexual abuse or sexual exploitation of a child.
- Sexual grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, and attempts to arrange sexual contact with a child are prohibited.
- Sexual extortion of a child, child sex trafficking, and any facilitation or encouragement of child sexual abuse or exploitation are prohibited.

## Report concerns in the app

If you see suspected CSAM, CSAE, or behavior that puts a child at risk, report it using Sociol’s in-app reporting tools.

- Open the relevant Circle and go to its information screen, where the members are listed.
- Tap the flag (Report) icon beside the member you want to report.
- Choose the most appropriate reason, add details explaining the child-safety concern, and tap Submit Report.

Include the account name, where the concern occurred, and relevant dates or times if available. You do not need to be certain that a violation occurred to report a concern. Do not download, copy, forward, or attach suspected CSAM to a report or email.

## How we respond

Sociol reviews child-safety reports and takes appropriate action based on the information available. This includes removing CSAM and other violating content, restricting or suspending accounts, and permanently banning users where appropriate.

When we become aware of CSAM on Sociol, we take action to remove it. We comply with applicable child-safety laws and report to relevant authorities where legally required. We may preserve relevant information as required by law and cooperate with lawful investigations.

## Child-safety contact

Sociol’s designated child-safety contact email is hello@sociol.net. Contact us about child-safety concerns, suspected CSAE or CSAM, or questions about these standards, including if you cannot use the in-app reporting tools.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.
